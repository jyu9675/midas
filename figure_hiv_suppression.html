Both taxa are lowest under viral suppression and highest in unsuppressed HIV — and the direction holds in both Boston and Botswana independently.

Requiring a signal to replicate across cohorts collapses an apparent 52-genus suppression effect to just two robust candidates — the rest is population structure, not ART response. A replication-first reanalysis, the same cross-validation rigor used in ML, applied to a clinical microbiome question.

Caveats: reanalysis of the authors' open processed 16S table, not new data; both taxa are low-abundance (~0.01–0.06%) with Greengenes placeholder names (sequence-level follow-up needed); the suppression contrast rests on 2 of 3 cohorts (Uganda lacks unsuppressed subjects). Genus-level, Mann–Whitney + BH-FDR; replication = q<0.1 in both cohorts, concordant direction. Hypothesis-generating.
