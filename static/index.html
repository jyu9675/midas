MiDAS is a resolver, not an archive. It sits on top of SRA/ENA/dbGaP and encodes the compatibility judgment an expert makes by hand — body-site match, shotgun vs 16S, and whether sequencing depth is enough for strain resolution (computed from ENA run-level bases, not scraped). Built for labs and institutes that generate deep multi-site microbiome data but lack a shared layer to find and reuse it.
